# Figure 1 | Model-adjusted HbA1c prevalence

**Title:** Diabetes-range HbA1c prevalence by income and educational attainment

**Caption:** Survey-weighted marginal prevalence of measured HbA1c >=6.5% among NHANES 2017-2018 adults aged 20 years or older, standardized to the covariate distribution of 4,362 complete-case respondents. Green circles indicate adjustment for age, sex, and the other socioeconomic variable; gray triangles add race/ethnicity. Vertical bars show approximate 95% confidence intervals from the survey-model covariance matrix and a t critical value with 15 nominal design degrees of freedom. Income categories are family income-to-poverty ratios. Results are descriptive and do not identify causal effects.

**Editorial guidance:** Clearly label the two specifications; do not characterize a confidence interval spanning zero in an *adjusted difference* as evidence of no association. The intervals plotted here describe prevalence estimates rather than direct between-group differences. The figure uses survey-standardized predictions, not observed subgroup prevalence. The accessible text alternative is in the integrated report and CSV tables.

**Values source:** `reports/16_model_comparison_prevalence.csv`. Contrasts are in `reports/16_model_comparison_contrasts.csv`.
