## LinkedIn project description
**Population Health Intelligence | Independent Epidemiology & Health Data Analytics Project**

I developed an exploratory population health analysis using the CDC's NHANES 2017–2018 public-use data to examine how income-to-poverty ratio and educational attainment relate to measured HbA1c >=6.5% among U.S. adults.

I used Python and R to merge and audit datasets, define a reproducible adult study cohort, apply NHANES complex survey weights and design variables, estimate prevalence with confidence intervals, fit survey-weighted logistic regression models, and calculate adjusted marginal prevalence. I also assessed incomplete income reporting and the sensitivity of findings to demographic adjustment.

The analysis included 5,569 adults in the initial study cohort and 4,362 complete-case participants in the adjusted models. Findings were descriptive: education-related differences were more apparent than income-related differences, but adjusted estimates depended on the model specification. The project includes a research report, figures, code, documented limitations, and automated reproducibility checks.
