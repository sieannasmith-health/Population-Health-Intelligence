# Final Portfolio Release Decision | October 15, 2026

**Release classification:** Exploratory, reproducible educational research portfolio case study. **Not** a peer-reviewed publication, clinical assessment, definitive population estimate, or causal study.

## Editorial decision
The integrated report's core estimates and selected figures have passed the documented internal checks. The two adjustment specifications are retained together. Confidence intervals and residual design degrees of freedom are described explicitly. Income nonresponse (791/5,569 adults), outcome missingness, complete-case selection and unobserved confounding remain meaningful limitations; they cannot be proven absent from these data. A full imputation/nonresponse sensitivity analysis and an external scientific review have not been performed. These are **limitations of the released exploratory analysis**, not tests certified as passed.

## Permitted claims
- Used public-use CDC NHANES 2017-2018 with an all-age survey design and an adult analytic domain.
- Produced weighted descriptive and adjusted standardized estimates and checked their local reproducibility.
- Evaluated observed differences by income missingness and model specification.
- Maintained public-safe source-control rules to exclude raw and processed person-level source data.

## Public release checks
The internal report review, visual figure inspection, Git candidate inspection and token-pattern checks are documented in reports 20-22. A fresh-folder rebuild passed on the originating Mac. The release may be posted as a *portfolio project* with these limitations; there is no assertion of independent scientific approval.

## Decision
**Approved for exploratory portfolio release only.** Any subsequent academic manuscript, journal submission or policy recommendation requires independent methodological review and additional nonresponse sensitivity work.
